🔋 SaldeerStop

A home battery and solar payback tool for the Dutch market, built around the end of net metering (saldering) on 1 January 2027. Developed as my own venture during the Minor Entrepreneurship at Hogeschool Utrecht.

🐍 Python ⚡ Energy Data 📊 Data Analysis 🚀 Entrepreneurship

The Problem

Until 2027, Dutch households with solar panels can offset the electricity they feed back into the grid against the electricity they use. From 1 January 2027 that scheme ends. Many solar owners will have to decide whether a home battery is worth it, and most have no reliable way to find out for their own situation.

The Idea

SaldeerStop is a sizing and payback tool for consumers and solar installers. It estimates whether a home battery saves money over its lifetime, and what size makes sense, for a specific household.

The business model: when the tool shows that a battery pays off, the household can be connected to a battery supplier or installer, who pays for that qualified lead.

Current Stage: Kill Test

Before building the product, I'm testing the assumption the whole idea rests on:

  • Hypothesis: Real household load profiles give a meaningfully different battery payback than the standard profiles most calculators use.
  • If true: A personalised tool has a clear advantage over generic calculators, and the venture moves forward.
  • If false: Generic calculators are good enough, and I pivot or stop.

What This Project Shows

  • Framing a data problem around a real regulatory and market change
  • Testing the riskiest assumption first instead of building straight away
  • Connecting a data product to a concrete revenue model
  • Working towards a graduation route via my own company
